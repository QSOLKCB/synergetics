# Claim Boundaries

This project compares five research stations:

1. Buckminster Fuller's Synergetics;
2. QSOL-IMC research and software;
3. demonstrably Synergetic structures in Brian Thorne's repositories;
4. Paul Hawken's regenerative and climate-action frameworks;
5. Buddhist Middle Way and Threefold Training concepts.

## This repository does not claim

- that Fuller anticipated E8, qutrit protocols, modern AI, optimal transport or Buddhist doctrine;
- that QSOL models implement Fuller's exact geometrical accounting;
- that Thorne's Fano, H4 or boundary systems are equivalent to Fuller's Synergetics;
- that Hawken's reciprocity is a vector equilibrium or a mathematical balance law;
- that the Buddhist Middle Way is an arithmetic midpoint, static equilibrium or compromise between every pair of views;
- that sīla, samādhi and paññā are qutrit states, software modules or vertices of a literal geometric triangle;
- that dependent arising is a universal licence to equate every relational theory;
- that spiritual cultivation can be reduced to system optimisation, deterministic replay or performance telemetry;
- that sonification proves a mathematical, ecological, metaphysical or spiritual claim;
- that a high field score demonstrates historical influence or shared ontology.

## Buddhist-source boundary

The Buddhist station is limited to comparative modelling of sourced concepts including:

- the Middle Way as avoidance of harmful extremes and the Noble Eightfold Path;
- the Threefold Training in ethical conduct, heightened mind/concentration and discernment;
- dependent arising as conditionality and non-isolation;
- a derived, explicitly non-canonical representation of reciprocal cultivation among the trainings.

The lab must preserve the distinct Buddhist aim of ending suffering. It must not relabel Buddhist training as merely psychological balance, productivity, engineering control or computational stability.

## Hawken-source boundary

The Hawken station distinguishes:

- Project Regeneration's life-centred renewal framework;
- Project Drawdown's science-based climate-solution assessment;
- distributed social action associated with *Blessed Unrest*.

Current Project Drawdown work should not automatically be attributed personally to Paul Hawken where institutional authorship has changed or broadened.

## Thorne-source boundary

Only inspectable software, specifications and geometric structures are included by default. Biblical, theological, consciousness and universal-ontology claims remain outside the strict mapping layer unless separately reviewed.

## QSOL-source boundary

QSOL parameters must preserve their own declared scientific limits. Algebraic dimension, graph state count, sonification output and visual projection do not imply physical spacetime dimensions or empirical validation.

## Interpretation rule

The centre of a field visualisation is a comparative abstraction. It is not a new universal theory created by averaging the selected sources.
